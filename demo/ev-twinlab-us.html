How the score weights were calibrated

  
Real data: the Twin-2K-500 Mega-Study. It covers 19 US studies, with AI "digital twins" of the same real participants built with 15 GPT-4.1 set-ups (280 validation exercises). Each exercise scores the three tests on half of the people and half of the questions. It then measures the true segment error on the other people answering the other questions, as if they were a new concept. The weights are the mix that best predicts that error.
